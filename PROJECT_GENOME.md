## Core Purpose

Cham.ai is a voice AI platform designed to combine:
- conversational AI
- telephony integration
- workflow orchestration
- business automation

Its role is to become a programmable voice interface for real-world operations.

---

## Strategic Goal

Cham.ai should evolve into a production-ready voice AI system capable of:
- receiving voice interactions
- understanding user intent
- routing tasks to the correct logic
- integrating with automation and operational systems
- serving as a foundation for future AI-driven communication products
